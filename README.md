# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI and the Financial Sample dataset. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, country-level performance, and product profitability through visualizations and interactive filters.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales across countries.
- Evaluate product-level profitability.
- Explore performance by year and customer segment.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data visualization
- Interactive dashboard development

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Display total sales and total profit.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Shows changes in sales throughout the year.
- **Sales by Country:** Compares sales performance across countries.
- **Profit by Product:** Compares profitability across products.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Allow users to filter results by year and customer segment.

## Key Insights

- **Overall Performance:** The dashboard reports total sales of 118.73M and total profit of 16.89M.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** October records the highest sales peak in the displayed monthly trend.
- **Country Performance:** The United States has the highest sales among the countries displayed.
- **Product Profitability:** Paseo generates the highest profit among the products shown in the dashboard.
- **Interactive Analysis:** Year and customer segment filters allow users to explore performance across different reporting periods and segments.

*Note: Sales and profit values are displayed in the units used by the source dataset.*
